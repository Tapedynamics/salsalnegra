Eating gluten-free in south Tenerife: what to know

For those seeking to enjoy the vibrant culinary scene of south Tenerife while maintaining a gluten-free diet, you're in for a treat. With the island's increasing awareness of dietary needs, finding gluten free restaurants in south Tenerife has become more accessible than ever. Whether you’re a celiac visiting Costa Adeje or just someone who prefers to avoid gluten, here’s what you need to know to relish your gastronomic journey without worry.

Understanding Naturally Gluten-Free Canarian Dishes

The Canarian diet, rich in fresh seafood, vegetables, and traditional preparations, offers several dishes that are inherently gluten-free. Exploring these options can be a delightful way to savor local flavors without compromising on dietary restrictions.

Papas Arrugadas

A quintessential part of Canarian cuisine, papas arrugadas are small, wrinkled potatoes boiled in heavily salted water. They are typically served with mojo sauces—one red and spicy, the other green and garlicky. Both sauces are usually made without gluten, but it's always a good idea to ask, as recipes can vary.

Grilled Fish and Seafood

Given Tenerife's coastal location, seafood is a staple. Grilled or baked fish, such as sea bass or dorada, is often prepared with simple seasonings, keeping it gluten-free. Accompanied by local vegetables or a fresh salad, these dishes are both delicious and safe for those avoiding gluten.

How to Inquire About Gluten-Free Options

When dining out, especially in an area like Costa Adeje, communication is key to ensuring a safe gluten-free experience. Here are a few tips on how to ask about gluten-free options effectively:

  • Learn Basic Phrases: Knowing a few phrases in Spanish can be incredibly helpful. Phrases like "¿Esto tiene gluten?" (Does this have gluten?) or "Soy celíaco" (I am celiac) can go a long way in communicating your needs.
  • Ask for the Menu: Many restaurants now offer gluten-free menus or mark gluten-free items on their regular menus. Don’t hesitate to ask if such options are available.
  • Speak to the Chef: If you’re unsure, ask if you can speak to the chef or a knowledgeable staff member. They can provide more detailed information about how dishes are prepared.

Identifying Safe Dining Establishments

While exploring gluten free restaurants in south Tenerife, it's crucial to identify places that prioritize cross-contamination precautions and cater to celiacs.

Check for Certifications

Some restaurants have certifications or affiliations with celiac organizations that confirm their commitment to gluten-free cooking. Look for signage or mention of such credentials on their website or menu.

Read Reviews

Online reviews can be a great resource for gauging the experience of other gluten-free diners. Websites, blogs, and social media platforms often provide insights into how well a restaurant caters to celiac customers.

Observe the Menu and Staff Knowledge

A comprehensive menu that clearly identifies gluten-free options, along with staff who are knowledgeable about dietary restrictions, can be a reassuring sign of a safe dining environment.

Conclusion

Eating gluten-free in south Tenerife is not only possible but can also be a rewarding culinary experience. From savoring naturally gluten-free Canarian dishes to finding dedicated gluten-free restaurants, there are plenty of options to explore.
