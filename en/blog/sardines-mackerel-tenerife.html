Blue fish in Tenerife: sardines, mackerel and why they are underrated

Blue fish such as sardines and mackerel play a significant role in the culinary traditions of Tenerife, yet they often remain underrated by visitors seeking more familiar seafood choices. Known for their rich nutritional profile, these fish are celebrated for their high omega-3 fatty acid content, which contributes to heart health and other benefits. Embracing these local flavors can provide a more authentic taste of the Canary Islands, offering a delightful combination of taste and health benefits.

Sardines and mackerel are more than just staple fish; they are integral to the Canary Islands' gastronomic heritage. Their versatility means they can be prepared in a variety of ways, from traditional grilling to the tangy escabeche method. Sadly, their humble reputation often leads to them being overshadowed by more glamorous seafood options. However, understanding their culinary value can enhance your dining experience in Tenerife, whether you're enjoying a meal at a local eatery or a fine dining restaurant like Sal Negra Tenerife.

Nutritional Value of Blue Fish

Blue fish, including sardines and mackerel, are often celebrated for their exceptional nutritional benefits. Packed with omega-3 fatty acids, they help reduce inflammation and improve cardiovascular health. They are also a great source of high-quality protein, vitamins B12 and D, and essential minerals such as selenium and iodine.

Incorporating these fish into your diet can contribute to improved brain function, stronger bones, and a healthier immune system. Given their health benefits, it's no surprise that blue fish should be a regular part of your diet, especially when visiting a region where they're freshly caught and expertly prepared.

Classic Preparation Techniques

Grilling (Plancha)

One of the most popular ways to enjoy blue fish in Tenerife is by grilling them, a method known locally as "a la plancha." This technique allows the natural flavors of the fish to shine through, enhanced by a touch of salt and a drizzle of olive oil. The result is a succulent, flavorful dish that's both simple and satisfying.

Escabeche

Another classic preparation method is escabeche, where the fish is marinated in vinegar, oil, and spices. This technique not only adds a unique tangy flavor but also helps preserve the fish, making it a popular choice for tapas. Escabeche can be enjoyed as a cold appetizer, offering a refreshing contrast to the warm climate of Tenerife.

Why Are They Underrated?

Despite their numerous benefits, sardines and mackerel are often overlooked. One reason might be their strong flavor, which may not appeal to everyone, especially those unaccustomed to the distinct taste of blue fish. Additionally, their reputation as "cheap" fish can lead to misconceptions about their quality or desirability.

Yet, when prepared correctly, these fish can be a culinary delight. Conclusion

While sardines and mackerel might not be the first fish that come to mind when you think of fine dining, their rich flavor and nutritional profile make them worth trying. Whether you're exploring the vibrant dining scene in Costa Adeje or savoring a meal at Sal Negra Tenerife, these blue fish offer a taste of the Canary Islands that's both authentic and healthful.
